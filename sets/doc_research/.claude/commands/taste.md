Show `doc_research/TASTE.md` — the rules the researcher has given about how you work — and, if
$ARGUMENTS says what to change, apply it.

Adding a rule: append one dated line under `## Rules`, in their words, specific enough to act on.
"Shorter messages" is fine; "be better" is not — ask what specifically.

Removing one: delete the line. It is genuinely forgotten; do not keep a note of it anywhere.

Remember that `TASTE.md` overrides `doc_research/CLAUDE.md` on style, length, format, plotting and
workflow. The only things it cannot switch off are the provenance markers and the honesty rules —
if that is what is being asked for, do not argue: write it into `doc_research/FEEDBACK.md` as a
sign that the shipped instructions are wrong here, and say it has been noted.
