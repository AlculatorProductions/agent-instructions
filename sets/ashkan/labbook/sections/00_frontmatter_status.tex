\section{Scope, Status, and Evidence Rules}
\label{sec:frontmatter-status}

\subsection*{What this document is}

This is the durable record of this research notebook. Working material --- brainstorms, reading
notes, quick derivations, individual simulation runs --- lives in the Markdown layer of the
repository (\texttt{ideas/}, \texttt{derivations/}, \texttt{runs/}, \texttt{literature/notes/}). A
result is written here only after it has been checked, and every entry names the checks that were
run.

Nothing in this document is established by having been written here. It is established by the
source, derivation, or run that the sentence points at.

\subsection*{Evidence tags}

Every factual sentence carries one of:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \evS{}{} --- stated by a source registered in \texttt{literature/SOURCES.md}, at the cited
        location;
  \item \evD{id} --- our derivation, checked, under \texttt{derivations/};
  \item \evC{run-id} --- our computation, recorded under \texttt{runs/};
  \item \evI{} --- our inference or interpretation: plausible, not yet checked;
  \item \evQ{} --- needs a source or a check.
\end{itemize}

An untagged factual sentence is a defect. The distinction that matters most is between \evS{}{} and
\evI{}: what a paper says, versus what we concluded from it.

\subsection*{Validation gates}

Results record which of the six gates actually ran, as \gates{S,D,M,C}:

\begin{description}
  \item[S --- Source] the source is local, hashed, and registered.
  \item[D --- Definition] terms are in \texttt{GLOSSARY.md}, conventions in \texttt{CONVENTIONS.md}.
  \item[M --- Math] a derivation or cited theorem supports the statement.
  \item[C --- Computation] a run checks an invariant, a known limit, or a published number ---
        independent of the statement it checks; a circular comparison is not a check.
  \item[R --- Review] independently reviewed.
  \item[B --- Build] \texttt{pixi run check}, \texttt{pixi run test}, \texttt{pixi run labbook} pass.
\end{description}

Naming a gate that was not run is the one unrecoverable error in this repository.
