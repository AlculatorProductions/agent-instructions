\section{Source Queue and Open Questions}
\label{sec:open-questions}

This shard is the honest inventory of what is missing. It is expected to be long. A short version
of this section means the notebook has stopped asking questions, not that it has answered them.

\subsection*{Source queue}

\status{open} Sources identified as needed but not yet acquired and registered. Each row: what is
needed, what it would settle, and where it is expected to be found. A source stays here until it
has a local PDF, a hash, and an entry in \texttt{literature/SOURCES.md}. None queued yet.

\subsection*{Unverified claims}

Claims carrying \evQ{} anywhere in the repository are the verification backlog. They are found
with

\begin{verbatim}
grep -rn '\[?\]' --include='*.md' --include='*.tex' .
\end{verbatim}

\noindent and their \LaTeX{} counterparts with \verb|grep -rn 'evQ' labbook/|. Claims carrying
\evI{} are our own unchecked reasoning and are the second queue; promoting one to \evS{}{} requires
opening the source and reading the passage, never inference from an abstract or a summary.

\subsection*{Open questions}

\status{open} The questions the notebook exists to answer, in the form: the question, why it
matters, and what an answer would have to look like. Questions are moved out of this list only when
they are answered (with the shard that answers them) or explicitly abandoned (with the
\texttt{attic/} entry that records why). None recorded yet.
