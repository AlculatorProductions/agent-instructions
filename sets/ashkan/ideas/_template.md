# Title

## The question

What exactly is being asked. One paragraph. If it takes three, there are probably two ideas here —
split them.

## Why it might be true

The reasoning, tagged honestly: `[S:Key §2]` for what a source says, `[I]` for our own steps,
`[?]` for the bits we are recalling rather than checking.

## What would confirm it

The concrete thing — a derivation, a limiting case, a simulation with a specific observable, a
number to compare against a published one. Be specific enough that someone else could go do it.

## What would kill it

The observation, calculation or known result that would rule it out. If nothing would, say so —
that is a finding about the idea.

## Assumptions

What has to hold for this to make sense. Mark which are checked (`[S:...]`, `[D:...]`) and which
are assumed (`[I]`).
